                                                                                    Task - I 

Analyze the business requirements of the E-Commerce Order Management System and prepare a requirement specification document.

Amazon Website

1. Introduction

Amazon is one of the world's largest e-commerce websites. It allows users to buy products online, make secure payments, and track their orders easily.

2. Functional Requirements

- User Registration and Login
- Search and Filter Products
- Add to Cart and Wishlist
- Secure Online Payment
- Order Tracking
- Customer Reviews and Ratings

3. Non-Functional Requirements

- Fast Loading Speed
- High Security for User Data and Payments
- Easy-to-Use Interface
- Reliable System Availability
- Scalable to Handle Millions of Users

4. Conclusion

Amazon is a reliable and user-friendly e-commerce platform that provides a secure and convenient online shopping experience with efficient features and high performance.
